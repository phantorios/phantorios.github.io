AKS GitOps Platform

in progress

A Kubernetes platform on Azure, provisioned with Terraform and reconciled by ArgoCD. Every credential in the chain is federated, so the repositories store no secret at all.

  • GitHub to AzureOIDC federated credential. No service principal secret is stored.
  • AKS to ACRManaged identity pulls images. No imagePullSecret is stored.
  • pod to AzureWorkload identity. No connection string is stored.

Six design decisions are recorded as ADRs, including why OIDC replaced stored credentials, and why environments are separate tfvars rather than branches.

Terraform · AKS · ArgoCD · GitHub Actions · OIDC
